About

I am a PhD candidate in Operations Management at Ivey Business School, Western University, and a visiting PhD student at the Fisher College of Business at The Ohio State University.

My research examines how firms build more responsible buyer–supplier relationships, with a particular focus on supplier-directed prosocial behavior and supply chain transparency. I am interested in understanding how buying firms can better understand and support their suppliers, and how firms' disclosure of upstream supply chain relationships shapes reputational outcomes.

My research draws on multiple methods, including archival panel data analysis, qualitative case research, and AI-enabled text analysis. My earlier background in industrial engineering, mathematical modeling, and industry experience in process improvement also informs how I approach real-world operational problems.
