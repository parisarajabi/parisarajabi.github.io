About

I am a PhD Candidate in Operations Management at Ivey Business School, Western University. My research examines questions at the intersection of operations management, supply chain management, sustainability, and organizational decision making.

My work primarily uses empirical methods to study how firms design, govern, and communicate their operational and supply chain practices. I am particularly interested in questions related to supply chain transparency, sustainability, and responsible operations.
